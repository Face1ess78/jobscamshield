Three essential safety rules

  1. 01
    Never pay to get hired.

    Registration, training, equipment, and joining fees are all warning signs.

  2. 02
    Keep financial information private.

    No recruiter needs your OTP, PIN, password, or payment-card details.

  3. 03
    Verify independently.

    Find the employer’s official site yourself; don’t rely on a recruiter’s link.
